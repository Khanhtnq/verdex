### 09:44 (07:44 UTC) · `clip-lendv3.mp4`

> What changed in Lend v3.
>
> Our own audit found what to fix before any liquidity: the oracle quietly fell back to spot when the pool's observations were short (manipulable on a sub-second chain), liquidations priced collateral at the higher of spot and TWAP (unprofitable during a real drop), a position that ran out of collateral left phantom debt forever, and the short markets' reserves were sent somewhere that could not use them. All fixed in the repo, re-tested on a fork, and the contract going live today is that code.
>
> The audit is public: docs/audit-2026-10-05.md.
>
> useverdex.xyz/docs#audit
